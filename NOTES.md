## What it is

A market-making bot that quotes both sides of the order book and earns the spread, with market making, arbitrage, cross-exchange hedging, grid, DCA and TWAP strategies across centralised and decentralised venues.

## Caution

Market makers get paid for carrying inventory risk, so when the market runs one way you are the one holding the bag. Paper first, then tiny size.

## Status

Forked Oct 27, 2026. Nothing here has been run, configured, or connected to any account, API key, or real money. Paper / dry-run first, and only after a walk-forward backtest with fees, a look-ahead check, and 30 days on paper.
